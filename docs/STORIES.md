# Shared paragraph flow

Stories retain original rich paragraphs and an ordered list of flow slots. Independent `story_frame` items place those slots in a vector or raster document. Moving or transforming a placement leaves source flow order unchanged. Editing a shared story updates all its placements; copying a story under another ID creates an independent source.

This implementation covers threading, columns, spacing, structural lists, visible rule-driven hyphenation, named paints, source mapping and persistence. Independent counter, layout, pixel and history evidence covers the unchanged `vector.paragraphs.extended` acceptance. The separate [hyphenation inspection](HYPHENATION.md) operation exposes the same explicit weighted-rule semantics without layout.

## Model and edits

`document.stories` maps stable IDs to `{paragraphs,slots,overset,hyphenation_rules}`. The rule table defaults to empty. Each paragraph has `text`, `style`, optional character `ranges`, `align`, `direction`, `bidi`, `leading`, `before`, `after`, `indent_start`, `indent_end`, `first_indent`, optional `list` and optional `hyphenation`. The style/font/range contracts are those of [editable Unicode text](UNICODE_TEXT.md). Paragraphs are separate records; embedded LF is rejected. Empty paragraphs consume one font line cell.

Each slot has a unique `id`, finite positive `width` and `height`, `columns` (default 1), `gutter` (default 0) and `reverse_columns` (default false). Columns have equal widths after subtracting gutters. Their default order is left to right. The slot array defines flow order independently of slot IDs, item order, transforms and paragraph direction.

Place a slot using `content:{type:"story_frame",story_id:"article",slot_id:"first"}`. Multiple items can refer to one slot. A slot without a placement still participates in source flow, making artboard extraction and partial transfer stable. Bounds are the selected slot rectangle; glyph ink is separately inspectable.

Atomic edits use `op:"story"`, `id` and `story` to insert or replace a whole story. Set `story:null` to remove an unreferenced story. `op:"story_range"` accepts the story `id`, zero-based `paragraph`, scalar `start`/`end`, and optional replacement `text` and/or `style`. Ranges must follow grapheme boundaries. Original documents remain unchanged; session undo/redo and retry receipts retain ordinary revision semantics.

Story edits and font replacement check every affected placement and its component/mask dependents for locks. Removing a referenced story or a font retained by any story fails. Duplicating a placement shares its source. Explicit story copying preserves independence. Transfer copies the complete source story and all its ordered slots, remaps story/font IDs and can verify external font resources without modifying them.

## Layout and source accounting

Without hyphenation, line fitting checks the longest fitting reshaped grapheme prefix, preferring an earlier standalone ASCII space when the complete remaining paragraph does not fit. Enabled hyphenation uses the legal candidate policy below. Shaping may change advances with context, so fit does not assume monotonic widths. Spaces skipped at a wrapped boundary remain in the source and are included in `consumed_end`.

Full paragraph direction analysis survives column and slot boundaries. Each selected line is shaped in its line-local context. Logical start/end indents follow the paragraph's base direction; the signed first-line indent is added to `indent_start` and cannot extend before the column. Column order remains explicitly controlled by the slot.

The line cell is maximum ascent plus maximum descent plus maximum nonnegative line gap across the base style and actual resolved glyph fonts. Explicit leading controls the next line's top, so smaller leading may overlap line cells intentionally. Before/after spacing is additive and does not collapse. Before spacing applies at the first placed line even after a column break. After spacing affects the next paragraph; trailing after-space alone does not create an overset tail.

If the next line or grapheme cannot fit, flow tries the next column/slot without consuming its source. Indents that leave no width also skip that column. A glyph's overhang is reported by the slot layout's `overflowed` flag. Every placement clips to its whole slot rectangle; columns do not add separate ink clips.

`overset:"error"` is the default: rendering, vector delivery and outline conversion fail with `STORY_OVERFLOW` when text remains. Explicit `overset:"retain"` renders the placed portion and keeps the complete original source. Inspection always reports the first unplaced `{paragraph,offset}` or `null`, regardless of delivery policy. A slot without an item is intentional unplaced artwork, not an overset source condition.

`story.inspect` takes a document, story ID, explicit font root and optional `include_outlines`. It returns the retained story and layouts for every slot. Its ordered `lines` identify slot, visual column, local layout line index, paragraph, source `start`/`end`, `consumed_end`, and glyph-index range. Offsets are **paragraph-local Unicode scalars**, not bytes. Combined with `overset`, these records account for consumed and remaining text. `text.inspect` on a story-frame item returns that slot's layout and source records. `document.inspect` and structural diffs expose story bindings and source changes.

## Lists and generated source

A paragraph's optional `list` has a story-local `id`, zero-based `level` (default 0), `marker`, optional `restart`, `gap` (default 0), and optional complete marker `style` (defaults to the paragraph base style). Lists use stable IDs independently of item IDs and placement order. A bullet marker is `{type:"bullet",text:"…"}`. Ordered markers use `{type:"ordered",format:"decimal",prefix:"",suffix:"."}`; the prefix and suffix shown are defaults. Other formats are `lower_alpha`, `upper_alpha`, `lower_roman` and `upper_roman`. Alphabetic numbering continues from Z to AA; Roman numbering uses subtractive forms IV, IX, XL, XC, CD and CM. Prefix/suffix are literal text, with no interpolation or formatting language.

Each list ID retains counters across ordinary paragraphs and interleaved other lists. Every listed paragraph, including an empty item or bullet, advances its level's counter. A level starts at 1 unless `restart` is explicit; an existing level increments unless restarted. Visiting a level discards its deeper counters, so a new child sequence starts at 1. Nested items require an earlier parent at every lower level. Switching the marker format leaves the counter sequence intact. Nesting determines counters; agents explicitly choose paragraph indentation. Counter vectors are inspectable, while displayed numbers use the current level only.

Markers appear exactly once at the first placed line of an item, even when the paragraph continues through several columns or frames. They are shaped separately with the paragraph's resolved base direction and bidi mode, leaving the body's direction analysis and original text unchanged. Choose `bidi:"unicode"` for mixed-script marker order. The first-line logical start edge anchors the marker: its advance ends `gap` before that edge in LTR paragraphs, or starts `gap` beyond it in RTL paragraphs. Body alignment operates independently within its available width. The first-line indent must accommodate marker advance plus gap; otherwise the line tries the next column/slot and ultimately reports overset. Marker ink overhang follows the ordinary whole-slot clip/overflow contract. Marker fonts participate in the first line's ascent/descent/gap maxima.

`SourceLine.glyph_start/glyph_end` identify body display glyphs, including any generated hyphen but excluding list markers. Its optional `marker` supplies list ID, level, full counter vector, displayed text, x/advance, marker glyph interval and optional marker-local bidi analysis. Marker glyphs follow body glyphs in paint order; their ordinary `start` and `end` are both 0, and `generated:{type:"list_marker",start,end}` supplies **marker-local scalar offsets**. They never consume a body character. Empty listed paragraphs therefore have an empty body interval and a separate nonempty marker interval. This distinction survives `story.inspect` and `text.inspect`; snapshots retain the list specification and regenerate markers deterministically.

Change numbering, labels or marker styles with a whole `story` edit. `story_range` modifies only body text/style. Marker styles participate in font resolution, dependency locks, removal guards, transfer remapping and resource inspection. Shared source edits, independent story copies, snapshots, undo/redo, SVG/PDF outlines and rendered images retain ordinary story contracts. Labels are not editable document text in outlined formats.

Lists permit 64 independent IDs per story, levels 0–8, counters/restarts 1–999,999, and Roman values 1–3,999. Bullet text and each affix permit at most 32 Unicode scalars without controls, bidi marks or line/paragraph separators. Bullets must contain non-whitespace text. Gaps are finite 0–32,768. Invalid nesting, counter overflow, invalid literals and invalid gaps fail with `INVALID_LIST`. Both body text and all derived marker characters count toward the shared 4,096-character validation budget, even in unplaced/overset source. Marker paint and computed glyph/outline work also count toward shared budgets.

## Rendered hyphenation

Store named weighted rule sets in a story's `hyphenation_rules`, using the [Rules contract](HYPHENATION.md). Enable a paragraph with `hyphenation:{rules_id:"words",mark:"hyphen_minus",emergency_break:false}`. The mark and emergency policy shown are defaults. `mark:"hyphen"` uses U+2010 instead of U+002D. Rule IDs are local to the story; copies and transfer retain complete independent rule tables. Whole-story edits update rules; source-range edits leave them intact.

The pinned Unicode word segmenter selects lexical tokens, including their exact source bytes. Whitespace and punctuation outside those tokens remain in source. Word-local rule positions are mapped back to paragraph-local scalar positions; grapheme boundaries and minima remain those of each complete token. No language or dictionary is guessed. An exception key must match the selected token, not its surrounding punctuation.

If the remaining paragraph fits, no hyphen is inserted. Otherwise the engine checks permitted ASCII-space boundaries and rule opportunities from furthest to nearest, measuring every candidate as shaped text. A hyphen candidate temporarily inserts the selected mark at the boundary in a copy of the complete paragraph. The mark inherits the preceding source character's complete style and font preferences. Full-paragraph script/bidi analysis and line-local shaping then measure the fragment **including** the hyphen, so kerning, ligatures, fallback, tracking and contextual forms affect fit. This temporary view never changes stored text or ranges. The next line starts at the same original scalar boundary.

If no legal fragment fits, flow tries the next column/slot and eventually reports overset. `emergency_break:true` explicitly permits the ordinary unmarked grapheme/space fallback in that case. It does not suppress invalid rule data, missing glyphs or resource errors. The engine makes greedy line choices, not global paragraph optimization or full Unicode line-breaking conformance. Dictionary accuracy, spelling-changing hyphenation, automatic dictionaries, input soft-hyphen controls, arbitrary hyphen marks and continuation-prefix conventions are not implemented. Enabled paragraphs containing U+00AD fail explicitly; callers can express discretionary positions as rule exceptions while retaining their intended source text separately.

Each hyphenated `SourceLine` has `hyphen:{offset,character,word_start,word_end,rules_id,rules_sha256,display_bidi?}`. Its source `start`, `end` and `consumed_end` stay in the original paragraph. A glyph cluster containing the inserted mark has `generated:{type:"hyphen",offset}`. Ordinary glyph `start/end` map to original source: a pure inserted glyph has `[offset,offset)`, while a ligature combining source letters and the mark retains its nonempty source range plus generated provenance. This identifies mixed clusters without falsely claiming one glyph per source character.

The ordinary line's bidi record describes its original source interval. `hyphen.display_bidi` describes the actual display candidate, with one virtual scalar inserted at `offset` and a display interval ending at `offset+1`. Display glyph levels/directions come from that virtual analysis. All source positions after the insertion would be shifted by one in that diagnostic view; retained source offsets and subsequent line cursors never shift. This explicitly exposes any weak-character resolution changed by the displayed hyphen.

There are at most 16 rule sets per story. Across the entire document, retained rules share limits of 32,768 patterns, 262,144 pattern scalars, 4,096 exceptions and 65,536 exception-key scalars. Individual token/pattern limits remain those of the matcher. The 768 KiB snapshot and 1 MiB CLI limits can reject a document before these rule limits. Paragraphs with more than 256 words are processed in batches sharing the story's 4,194,304 matching-work budget. Candidate full-paragraph analysis and glyph shaping share the 1,048,576 shaping-work budget. Only internal temporary bidi analysis permits the one extra generated scalar beyond 4,096 source scalars. Generated glyphs and paths count toward ordinary layout/delivery budgets.

## Named paints

Body, character-range and list-marker styles accept named paints; generated hyphens inherit the preceding source paint. Display previews, tint chains, palette inspection, native PDF ink operands, overprint diagnostics, locks, diffs and cross-document resource remapping use the shared [swatch contract](SWATCHES.md). Unplaced retained stories prevent deletion of their referenced colors but do not force previews or font loading during unrelated rendering. Item dependencies conservatively include the complete bound story, even if a particular color appears in another slot.

`swatch_bake` makes selected story bindings independent when other bindings must retain live colors. If all direct bindings are selected, it bakes that source in place. A copied story preserves all paragraphs, ordered slots, fonts, lists and hyphenation rules; source/resource budgets still apply. Unselected live bindings and their locks remain intact. Ordinary shared component definitions retain their existing explicit selection semantics.

## Delivery and limits

PNG/JPEG/TIFF rendering uses the shared glyph paths, with the selected slot clip. SVG/PDF and `text_outline` deliver ordinary glyph outlines. Snapshots retain complete editable stories; outlined/page formats do not recreate editable stories on import. Artboard views retain ordered source slots even when their placement items are outside the selected board. Components, artwork masks, ordinary transforms, clipping and opacity use the same placements.

Limits are 32 stories per document, 256 paragraphs and 64 slots per story, 16 columns per slot, and 4,096 body plus generated marker characters across all stories and ordinary text. Shared placements do not duplicate source character storage. Glyph/outline budgets cover both computed story layouts and delivered placements; existing document, paint, font and request limits also apply. Extents, gutters, before/after spacing and logical indents are bounded by 32,768 units; explicit leading retains the ordinary 0.001–4,096 contract. Invalid and unsupported semantics fail explicitly.

Dataset text bindings target ordinary text frames; story source edits use `story_range`. Vertical text, arbitrary polygon flow regions and balanced columns are not implemented. Interchange formats that outline text preserve appearance, not editable paragraph reconstruction; broader page/native interchange requirements remain separately tracked.
